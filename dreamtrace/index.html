隐私政策

梦迹 DreamTrace · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日

梦迹 DreamTrace 由个人开发者提供。本政策说明使用本应用时信息如何被处理。应用内的文本与本页一致。联系邮箱：litianxing2001@gmail.com。

我们是谁

本应用没有账号，也没有开发者自己的服务器。

留在这台手机上的信息

梦境文字、录音、解读、收藏、情绪和梦的类型，保存在这台手机的应用沙盒里。你如果填写生日，本应用只保存月和日，不保存年份，并且只用来在本机显示星座原型笔记。你如果记录昨晚，就寝时间、起床时间和睡眠感受也只保存在本机。语言、外观、字号、语音识别质量、晨间提醒开关和静音自动结束，同样只保存在本机。开发者收不到这些内容。

麦克风与语音

只有你按下录音时才会使用麦克风。转写优先在本机完成。如果这台手机的系统不能在本机识别当前语言，iOS 可能会把这一段语音发给 Apple，只为了把文字返回到本机。开发者不会收到、保存或出售这段语音。你可以不录音，在编辑页直接打字。

通知

晨间提醒是这台手机自己定时发出的本地通知。没有推送服务器，通知里也没有从网上拉来的内容。你可以在本应用或系统设置里关闭它。

导出与分享

只有你自己点导出或分享时，系统才会把一份文件交给你选中的功能，例如文件、邮件或信息。开发者不会收到这份文件。

我们不做的事

没有广告，没有第三方分析或跟踪工具，不追踪你在其他应用或网站上的活动，也不出售个人信息。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。本应用没有账号，记录留在设备上。

保存与删除

数据会留在这台手机上，直到你在设置里清空全部数据，或删除本应用。开发者没有副本，因此无法替你恢复已删除的内容。

你的选择

你可以在本机查看、导出和删除自己的记录，也可以不填写生日、不开启提醒、不使用麦克风。如果你所在地区的法律给予你额外权利，那些权利仍然有效。因为开发者不持有你的梦境副本，查阅和删除请在这台手机上完成。

政策更新

如果本政策有更新，生效日期会改，更新后的文本会同时出现在本应用和本网页。

Privacy Policy

DreamTrace · Effective date: October 18, 2026

DreamTrace is offered by an individual developer. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. The text in the app matches this page. Contact: litianxing2001@gmail.com.

Who we are

The app has no account and no developer-operated server.

What stays on this phone

Dream text, recordings, readings, favorites, mood, and dream type stay in the app’s sandbox on this phone. If you enter a birthday, the app stores only the month and day, not the year, and uses them only to show a sign note on this device. If you note last night, bedtime, wake time, and how you slept also stay on this device. Language, appearance, text size, speech-recognition quality, the morning reminder switch, and auto-stop stay on this device as well. The developer does not receive any of this.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only while you are recording. Transcription prefers this phone. If this phone’s system cannot recognize the current language on device, iOS may send that recording to Apple solely to return the text. The developer does not receive, keep, or sell that audio. You can skip recording and type in the editor.

Notifications

The morning reminder is a local notification scheduled on this phone. There is no push server, and the notification does not fetch remote content. You can turn it off in the app or in system settings.

Export and sharing

Only when you export or share does the system hand a file to the destination you choose, such as Files, Mail, or Messages. The developer does not receive that file.

What we do not do

There are no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, no tracking of your activity in other apps or on websites, and no sale of personal information.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account. Records stay on the device.

How long it stays, and deletion

Data remains on this phone until you erase it in Settings or delete the app. The developer has no copy, and cannot restore what you delete.

Your choices

On this phone you can view, export, and delete your records. You can also skip birthday, reminders, and the microphone. If the law where you live gives you additional rights, those rights remain. Because the developer does not hold a copy of your dreams, access and deletion happen on this phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date will change, and the updated text will appear both in the app and on this page.
